# LinkShelf Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

LinkShelf saves the links you choose to save. That is all it does with your data.

## What is stored

- The links you save, their titles, type (Reel, Post, Video…) and the time you saved them.
- Your app settings (for example, whether the floating button is on).

All of this is stored **only on your device**. LinkShelf has no account system and no servers,
and it does not collect analytics, advertising IDs or crash reports.

## Network use

- To show thumbnails and titles for YouTube links, the app requests them from YouTube
  (`i.ytimg.com` and `youtube.com/oembed`). Only the link you saved is sent.
- No other data leaves your device.

## Clipboard

The app reads the clipboard **only** when you tap the floating button, the Quick Settings tile
or the "Paste link" button, so it can save the link you copied. It never reads the clipboard in the background.

## Permissions

- **Display over other apps:** shows the floating save button. Optional.
- **Notifications:** shows a notification while the floating button is on. Optional.
- **Internet:** loads YouTube thumbnails and titles.

## Your control

Uninstalling the app deletes all saved data. You can also delete everything from Settings → Delete all links.
